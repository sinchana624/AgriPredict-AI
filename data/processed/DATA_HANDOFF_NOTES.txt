AGRIPREDICT AI - DATA HANDOFF NOTES
===================================

Dataset:
exploratory_model_dataset_for_handoff.csv

Coverage:
1991-2024 (34 years)
3 crops, 102 rows

Included data:
- Crop production, harvested area and yield
- Monthly and annual rainfall
- Monthly and annual maximum temperature
- Monthly and annual mean temperature
- Monthly and annual minimum temperature
- Annual pesticide use

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS:
1. Crop and pesticide figures are India-wide.
2. Weather data represent a regional grid covering approximately
   6-16 degrees North and 78-88 degrees East.
3. The geographic scopes do not match.
4. This dataset is exploratory only. It is NOT validated for
   nationwide crop-yield prediction.
5. Do not describe the regional weather as all-India weather.
6. Inspect source definitions, units and methodology before
   training a model or presenting results.

Original source file:
